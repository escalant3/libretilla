Libretilla

A small, distraction-free word processor for the Mac whose native file format is .docx. Open a Word document, write, and your changes are saved back to that same file.

Principles

Minimal

A menu bar, one compact toolbar, a formatting bar and an optional ruler. No ribbon.

Small

One focused app with two dependencies. No AI, no cloud, no accounts, no telemetry.

Distraction-free

A clean page on a quiet canvas. There are no Save dialogs: edits are saved as you type.

Word-compatible

Files open and save without losing data. Parts Libretilla doesn't edit are written back byte for byte.

Native by design

  • Dark Mode follows the system, including the page. Printing and PDFs always come out on white paper.
  • System autosave and Versions: File ▸ Browse All Versions… brings back earlier states.
  • The system's writing tools: spell and grammar checking, autocorrect, and the native find & replace bar.
  • Window tabs, full screen, Open Recent, Print and the standard keyboard shortcuts.
  • VoiceOver labels throughout, with a headings rotor for moving around a document.
  • Sandboxed, with no network access. The one connection is the update check, which sends nothing about you or your documents.

Requirements

  • A Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later).
  • macOS 26 or later.

Open the DMG and drag Libretilla to Applications. It updates itself: Libretilla ▸ Check for Updates…

Libretilla autosaves over the file you open, so try it on a copy first, and keep backups.
